The **Today** screen (routed as `TimerScreen`) is the heart of TimeFlow. It shows the current date prominently and four animated progress bars that tell you exactly how much of each time period has elapsed.

## What the screen shows

At the top of the screen you see today's date displayed as the **month name**, **day number**, and **day of week** — for example, "March 20 Thursday". Below the date, four `ProgressCard` composables stack vertically, one for each time scale. Each card contains a short sentence like:

> "you're 73.5% through the year"

Below the progress cards, a tab row lets you switch between **Tasks** and **Events** for today. The task tab breaks down your items by priority (high, medium, low) so you can see at a glance how much is left.

## The 4 progress bars

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Day" icon="sun">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Week" icon="calendar-week">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Month" icon="calendar">
    Tracks the current day of the month relative to the total number of days in the month.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Year" icon="calendar-days">
    Tracks the current day of the year relative to the total days in the year (365 or 366).
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Calculation formulas

Each bar is calculated as a percentage using the following formulas from `TodayScreen.kt`:

```kotlin
// Day progress
val dayPercent = getDayProgressPercent()
// Internally: (secondsElapsed / totalSecondsInDay) * 100

// Week progress
val weekPercent = (dayOfWeek.toFloat() / 7f) * 100

// Month progress
val monthPercent = (dayOfMonth.toFloat() / maximumDayInMonth.toFloat()) * 100

// Year progress
val yearPercent = (dayOfTheYear.toFloat() / maximumDayOfYear.toFloat()) * 100
```

<Note>
  The day progress is the most granular — it updates in real time using elapsed seconds, not just the current hour.
</Note>

## Visual design

Each `ProgressCard` uses a Material 3 `LinearProgressIndicator` that animates smoothly as time advances. The progress bar color follows the app's Material You theme by default.

## Red warning state

When a progress value reaches **90% or higher**, the progress bar changes to the Material 3 `errorColor` (red). This gives you an immediate visual signal that you are running low on time for that period.

```kotlin
val barColor = if (percent >= 90f) MaterialTheme.colorScheme.error
               else MaterialTheme.colorScheme.primary
```

<Warning>
  A red progress bar means you have used 90% or more of that time period. For the day bar, this means less than 2.4 hours remain.
</Warning>

## Task and event summary

Below the progress bars, two tabs show your agenda for today:

- **Tasks** — lists tasks grouped or counted by priority: high, medium, and low
- **Events** — lists calendar events scheduled for today

This lets you correlate how much time you have left in the day with what still needs to be done.

## How to interpret the bars

| Bar | 50% means | 90% (red) means |
|-----|-----------|------------------|
| Day | Noon | ~21:36 or later |
| Week | Wednesday evening (day 3.5 of 7) | Saturday morning |
| Month | Mid-month | Last 3 days of the month |
| Year | Around July 2 | Around November 24 |

<Tip>
  Use the year bar as a long-term gut-check. If the year is already 70% gone and your annual goals are only 30% complete, it is time to reprioritize.
</Tip>
