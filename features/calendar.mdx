---
title: "Calendar"
description: "View and manage your tasks and events in a week or month calendar view, and quickly add new items from the calendar screen."
---

The **Calendar** screen (`CalenderScreen.kt`) gives you a visual overview of your tasks and events. You can switch between a compact week view and a full month view, navigate between dates, and add tasks or events directly from the calendar.

TimeFlow uses the [kizitonwose Calendar Compose](https://github.com/kizitonwose/Calendar) library (`com.kizitonwose.calendar:compose:2.7.0`) to render both calendar views.

## Week view and month view

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Week view">
    The week view (`weekCalender.kt`) displays a single row of 7 days. It is compact and ideal for focusing on the current week's agenda.

    - Scroll horizontally to move between weeks
    - Tap any day to select it and see that day's tasks and events below
    - The current day is highlighted automatically
    - Best for day-to-day planning when you want a tight, focused view
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Month view">
    The month view (`monthCalender.kt`) renders a traditional grid showing the full month.

    - Scroll vertically to move between months
    - Tap any day to select it and see that day's tasks and events below
    - The current day is highlighted automatically
    - Best for a high-level overview of how tasks and events are distributed across the month
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Switching between views

A toggle control at the top of the calendar screen lets you switch between **Week** and **Month** view. Your preference is saved automatically to **DataStore** so the app remembers it the next time you open the calendar.

```kotlin
// Saving the selected calendar type
saveSelectedCalenderType(type: Int)

// Reading it back as a reactive stream
readCalendarType(): Flow<Int>
```

<Note>
  `type` uses an integer to represent the view mode: `0` for week view and `1` for month view (or vice versa depending on the app's convention). The preference persists across app restarts.
</Note>

## Navigating between dates

- **Week view** — swipe left or right to move forward or backward by one week
- **Month view** — swipe up or down to move forward or backward by one month
- Tap the **today** button (if visible in the toolbar) to jump back to the current date

## Viewing tasks and events on a date

When you tap a day in either view, the tasks and events scheduled for that day appear in a list below the calendar grid. Items are grouped so you can see tasks separately from events.

## How to add a task from the calendar

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the calendar screen">
    Tap **Calendar** in the bottom navigation bar to open the calendar screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to the target date">
    Swipe to the week or month containing the date you want to add a task to, then tap that day to select it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap the FAB (+) button">
    Tap the **+** floating action button in the bottom-right corner. A rounded menu (`RoundedFabMenu`) expands with two options.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Add task or Add event">
    Select **Add task** to open the `AddTaskBottomSheet`, or **Add event** to open the event creation flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the task details">
    In the bottom sheet, enter the task name, set a priority (**High**, **Medium**, or **Low**), and confirm any date or time fields. The selected calendar date is pre-filled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the task">
    Tap **Save** (or the equivalent confirm action) to add the task. It immediately appears under the selected day in the calendar.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Tap a specific date before opening the FAB menu. The bottom sheet uses the selected date as the default, saving you from setting it manually.
</Tip>

## The FAB menu

The `RoundedFabMenu` expands from the **+** button and presents two actions:

| Action | What it opens |
|--------|---------------|
| **Add task** | `AddTaskBottomSheet` — a bottom sheet for creating a task |
| **Add event** | Event creation flow for scheduling a calendar event |

Tapping anywhere outside the expanded menu closes it without making a selection.
